# Certificate under section 63(4) of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023

Template for a document signed and sealed with Codevibe Sign. It follows the form in the
Schedule to the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (see section 63(4)(c)). Fill in every blank,
tick one option where a list is given, print, and sign. Attach the hash report and the
annexure.

This template is not legal advice. Ask your advocate whether it suits your case, who should
sign Part B, and whether it should be on stamp paper or sworn as an affidavit.

## ANNEXURE: description of the Codevibe Sign record

1. The record. File name: ______________________________. It is the PDF produced by
   Codevibe Sign when envelope ______________________________ titled
   "______________________________" was completed on ____________ (date). It holds the
   signed pages followed by a signing certificate.

2. How it was produced. The document was uploaded to the Codevibe Sign service at
   ______________________________ (sign.codevibe.in, or the address of a self-hosted
   install) and sent to the recipients listed on the certificate page. Each recipient opened
   a private link sent to them and, where set, entered a one-time code, then signed in their
   browser. When the last recipient acted, the service drew the signatures on the pages,
   added the certificate pages and applied a PAdES electronic seal over the whole file.

3. What the certificate pages record. For each recipient: name, masked email or phone, the
   identity check used, IP address, browser and the time of each action; the SHA-256 of the
   original file; and the head of a hash chain in which every recorded step carries a
   SHA-256 hash of the one before it.

4. Integrity. The seal covers every byte of the file. Any change after sealing breaks it.
   The seal was checked on ____________ (date) at ______________________________/verify,
   which reported: "______________________________". The seal certificate's SHA-256
   fingerprint is

   ________________________________________________________________

   (for sign.codevibe.in: 6B6E D7EC C18F C1B1 B6F5 514C 58C2 13B4 8840 528D 665D F007 0D63
   90AF 73DF 9C80).

5. Copy produced. The copy produced is: [ ] the PDF itself on ______________ (media)
   [ ] a printout of ______ pages of that PDF. Its SHA-256, given in Parts A and B, was
   computed with ______________________________ (tool), and the hash report is enclosed.

Enclosures: hash report; result of the verify page; printout of the certificate pages.
