# Installation help and hosting

Self-hosting is free, and [Self-host with Docker](/getting-started/docker) walks through it.
If you would rather not do it yourself, book installation help.

## Installation help

An experienced engineer installs Codevibe Sign on your server within 4 working hours of
getting access, for Rs 1,999 plus GST, paid online. You get a GST tax invoice.

Book it at [sign.codevibe.in/install-help](https://sign.codevibe.in/install-help). The form
has three parts:

1. **Who we talk to**: your name, phone and email.
2. **Who the invoice is for**: company or legal name, GSTIN (optional) and billing address.
3. **The server**: **Where is it?**, **About the server** and **When suits you?**

Then **Continue to payment**. The form never asks for a password or a key: access to the
server is arranged with you safely after booking. If the page says **Booking opens soon**,
payments are not open yet; write to [hello@codevibe.in](mailto:hello@codevibe.in) instead.

## A server to run it on

Need a server? Our VPS hosting partners give an extra 20% discount through our links, listed
under **Need a server?** on the installation help page and on the
[pricing section](https://sign.codevibe.in/#pricing). A Linux server with at least 2 GB of memory
and 10 GB of free disk is enough to run it.
