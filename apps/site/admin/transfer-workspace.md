# Transfer a workspace

A transfer moves a workspace to someone else's account, for example when a client takes over
the workspace you set up for them, or a company changes who pays. The workspace keeps its
documents, people and address; only the account it belongs to changes.

## Who can start one

Only the **account holder**, the owner whose plan the workspace is on. Any owner of the
workspace can cancel an open offer. See [Team and roles](/admin/team-and-roles).

## Before you start

- The new owner must already be in the workspace, or invited to it. Invite them from
  **Settings, Team** first.
- Their account must have room for one more workspace. The Free plan holds one, so a person on
  Free who already has a workspace must move to Cloud first. The app checks this when you
  offer and again when they accept.
- A workspace can have one open offer at a time.

## Offer it (current owner)

1. Open **Settings, Workspace** and find **Transfer ownership**.
2. Enter the **New owner's email**.
3. Tick **Leave the workspace when it moves** if you want to leave. Otherwise you stay on as an
   admin.
4. Press **Transfer workspace**, read what moves and what stays, then **Send transfer offer**.

They get an email and a notice in the app. While the offer is open you can
**Cancel transfer**.

## Accept it (new owner)

1. Open the link in the email, or **Review the offer** on the notice in the app.
2. Read the offer, then **Accept** or **Decline**.
3. To accept, confirm with **Your password**. If your account has no password, sign in again
   first (**Sign in again**); a sign-in from the last 15 minutes counts.

You must be signed in with the address the offer was sent to. If your plan has no room for
another workspace, the page says so with an **Upgrade** link, and the offer stays open until
it expires.

## What moves and what stays

| Moves with the workspace        | Stays with the previous account          |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| Envelopes and sealed documents  | Invoices already issued                  |
| Templates                       | Aadhaar eSign credits                    |
| Members and open invitations    | The Cloud subscription and its paid term |
| API keys and webhooks           |                                          |
| Brand, settings and the address |                                          |

When it is accepted:

- the new owner becomes an owner, and the workspace counts on their account and plan;
- the previous owner becomes an admin, or leaves if they chose to;
- billing admins become members, because they were trusted with the previous account's money.
  The new owner can make someone billing admin again.

## Expiry and history

An offer expires after 7 days without an answer, and nothing changes. Declined, cancelled and
expired offers change nothing either. Start a new one if needed.

**Ownership history**, under the transfer card, lists every step: who offered the workspace
and to whom, and whether it was accepted, declined, cancelled or expired. Emails go out when
an offer is made, accepted, declined or cancelled.
