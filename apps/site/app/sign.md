# Sign a document

This page is for the person who got a signing email, and for senders who want to know what
their recipients see. No account is needed to sign.

## Steps

1. **Open the link** in the email. It is private to you and stops working when the envelope
   expires, is withdrawn or is done.
2. **Agree to sign electronically**, then **Continue to the document**.
3. **Confirm it is you.** Press **Send me a code** and enter the 6-digit code from your email
   or text messages. A code works for 10 minutes and allows 5 tries; **Send a new code** gets
   another. Some senders skip this step.
4. **Fill in your fields.** The page takes you from one to the next.
5. **Finish and sign**, or **Approve** if you were asked to approve.

## Your signature

Choose how to make it:

- **Draw** with a finger, pen or mouse.
- **Type** your name and pick a style: **Flowing**, **Casual** or **Formal**. Names in
  Devanagari, Bengali, Gujarati, Gurmukhi, Kannada, Malayalam, Oriya, Tamil, Telugu and Arabic
  script are shown in a font for that script from your device, so type your name the way you
  write it.
- **Upload** a picture of your signature: PNG, JPEG or WebP under 5 MB. The background is
  removed.

## Decline

If something is wrong, choose **Decline**, give a reason and press **Decline to sign**. The
sender sees the reason and can send a corrected document.

## Aadhaar eSign

If the sender asked for Aadhaar eSign, the last step is **Continue to Aadhaar eSign**. You
review the document on Leegality's page and confirm with the OTP sent to the mobile number
linked to your Aadhaar, then come back here. If it fails or runs out of time, press
**Try again**. Codevibe Sign never sees your Aadhaar number.

## Afterwards

The thank-you page offers **Download your copy** and **Download the signing certificate**.
When everyone has signed, you also get the sealed PDF by email, attached or as a download link
that works for 30 days.

If the link shows a message instead of the document:

| Message                    | What to do                                                                |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| This link has expired      | Ask the sender to send it again.                                          |
| This request was withdrawn | The sender voided or recalled it; they will send a new one if needed.     |
| It is not your turn yet    | Someone before you has not signed; you get an email when it is your turn. |
| You have already signed    | Nothing more to do. Your copy comes by email when everyone is done.       |
| This link does not work    | Use the newest email from the sender, or ask them to send the link again. |
