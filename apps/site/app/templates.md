# Templates in the app

A template is a document you send often, such as an offer letter or an NDA, saved with its
roles and fields. Sending it later only needs the names and emails. To use templates from
your own product, see [Templates over the API](/guides/templates).

## Create a template

Open **Templates**, then **New template**. It takes four steps:

1. **Documents**: the PDFs, as when sending.
2. **Roles**: a placeholder for each person, such as "Employee" or "HR". Give each a
   **Role name**, what it **Does** (sign, approve or get a copy), its **Identity check** and
   the signing order. Aadhaar eSign is chosen when sending, not in a template.
3. **Fields**: place each role's fields, as when sending.
4. **Details**: a name (up to 120 characters) and a description (up to 500).

On the Free plan an account has 2 active templates across its workspaces; archived ones do
not count.

## Send from a template

Press **Use** on the template, fill in each role's name and email, then **Send now**. The envelope works like any other: see
[Send a document](/app/send#follow-it).

To send the same template to many people at once, use [Bulk send](/app/bulk-send).

## Change, copy or archive

- **Edit** changes the template. Envelopes already sent from it do not change.
- **Duplicate** makes a copy to change separately.
- **Archive** hides it from the list and frees a place on the Free plan. **Restore** it from
  the **Archived** tab.
