# Your account and sign-in

## Create an account

There is no sign-up form. An account is made the first time you sign in with an emailed link:

1. On the home page press **Start free**, or open **Sign in**.
2. Choose **Email me a link instead** and enter your work email.
3. Open the link in the email. It works once and only for 10 minutes.
4. Name your first workspace under **Create your workspace**, and set a password for your
   account in the same step. See [Workspaces](/admin/workspaces).

If someone invited you, the invitation email does the same job: open it, enter your name and
choose a password under **Create account and join**.

## Your password

Everyone who owns or joins a workspace has a password, so they can sign in even when email
links are slow. You are asked for one once, in the step where you create your first workspace
or accept your first invitation, unless you already have one or sign in with Google. Type it
twice; the bar under it shows how strong it looks. Sign-in links keep working as well.

A password has 10 to 128 characters. Common passwords, keyboard runs and passwords made from
your email or name are refused. A few unrelated words work well.

## Sign in

- **Password:** enter your **Work email** and **Password**, then **Sign in**. After 5 wrong
  passwords in 15 minutes, sign-in pauses and the page says how many minutes to wait.
- **Emailed link:** **Email me a link instead**. One address can ask for at most 5 links in
  15 minutes.
- **Google:** **Continue with Google**, shown only when the install has Google sign-in set up.

## Staying signed in

- **Idle sign-out.** After 30 minutes without using Codevibe Sign in any tab, you are signed
  out. Two minutes before, a box says **You'll be signed out in 2:00 for your security**: press
  **Stay signed in** to carry on, or **Sign out**. Use in any open tab counts, so a tab you
  left in the background does not sign you out while you work in another.
- **Daily sign-in.** However busy you are, each sign-in lasts at most 12 hours.
- **Coming back.** The sign-in page says why you were signed out. Sign in and you return to the
  page you were on. A draft you were sending is saved before the sign-out.

Your install may use other times; its operator sets them. People signing a document from an
emailed link are never signed out this way.

Some installs check that a person rather than a script is signing in. Most people never see
it; when the check is unsure it shows a box to tick above the button.

## Forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page choose **Forgot password?**, then **Email me a reset link**.
2. The link works once, for 30 minutes. Enter a **New password** and **Save new password**.

A reset signs you out everywhere. Sign in again with the new password.

## Your name

Your name appears to your team, on the certificate page and in the audit trail of the
documents you send. Until you add one, they show your email address, and the app shows an
**Add your name** banner. A name has 2 to 80 characters and at least one letter.

## Personal settings

Open **Settings, Personal**, or **Personal settings** in the menu under your name. Password
and sessions are under **Security**.

| Setting                                | What it does                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Your name**                          | As above.                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Theme**                              | Light, Dark, or **Match my device**. The app starts in light.                                                                                                          |
| **Time zone**                          | The zone the app shows times in. Dates printed on documents follow the workspace's zone, not yours.                                                                    |
| **Set password** / **Change password** | Add a password to an account made from a link, or change it with your current one. **Sign out my other devices** is on by default. Every change is confirmed by email. |
| **Sign out other sessions**            | Ends every session except this one.                                                                                                                                    |
| **Sign out of all devices**            | Ends every session, this one too, after you confirm. Use it if a device is lost or someone else may have used your account.                                            |
