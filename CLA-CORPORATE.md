# Codevibe Sign Corporate Contributor Licence Agreement

> **Draft, for legal review.** This text has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer and may change
> before it is final. Version 1 (draft).

This agreement is for an organisation whose employees or contractors contribute to Codevibe
Sign as part of their work. It gives Codevibe Technology Private Limited ("Codevibe") the same
rights as the [individual agreement](CLA.md), for everyone the organisation names.

## 1. Who is covered

"The Organisation" is the legal entity named at the end of this agreement, together with the
entities it controls. "Designated Contributors" are the people it lists by GitHub user name in
the schedule, as updated from time to time by its contact person.

## 2. Grant

The Organisation agrees to sections 1 to 10 of the Codevibe Sign Individual Contributor Licence
Agreement, read with "You" meaning the Organisation, for every Contribution made by a
Designated Contributor in which the Organisation holds rights. This includes the copyright
licence, the right to relicense under any terms (open source or commercial), the patent licence
and Codevibe's promise to keep accepted Contributions available under the project's open-source
licence.

## 3. What the Organisation confirms

The Organisation confirms that:

1. the person signing has the authority to bind it;
2. each Designated Contributor is authorised to submit Contributions on its behalf;
3. it will tell Codevibe when a person should be added to or removed from the schedule. A
   removal applies to Contributions made after Codevibe receives the notice.

## 4. How to sign

Fill in the details below and send the signed agreement to hello@codevibe.in. Codevibe then adds
the Designated Contributors to the CLA Assistant allowlist, so their pull requests pass the CLA
check without a separate comment.

```
Organisation (legal name):
Registered address:
Contact person, name and email:

Designated Contributors (GitHub user names):

Signed for the Organisation
Name:
Title:
Date:
Signature:
```
