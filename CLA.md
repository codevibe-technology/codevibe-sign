Codevibe Technology Private Limited ("Codevibe") so that it can publish your contribution as
part of the project. You keep the ownership of everything you contribute.

Sign it once, by posting the comment the CLA Assistant asks for on your first pull request. If
you contribute as part of your job, or your employer has rights in what you write, your employer
signs the [corporate agreement](CLA-CORPORATE.md) instead.

https://github.com/codevibe-technology/codevibe-sign and the packages, applications and
documentation published from it.

## 11. How to sign

Post this comment on your pull request, exactly as written:

```

```

The CLA Assistant records your GitHub user name, the pull request and the time with the other
signatures in this repository. One signature covers all your Contributions, past and future.
